# T2, the typing environment: status

Branch `flambda2-rocq/T2-env`, on T1's `flambda2-rocq/T1-grammar`, with
T3's commits for the aliases picked on top (below). Files, all in
`middle_end/flambda2/types/rocq/`:

- `FlambdaBindingTime.v`: binding times and name modes. Written by T3
  as the first commit of its branch, owned by T2;
  this branch carries the same commit by cherry-pick.
- `FlambdaTypingEnv.v`: the environment and its functions, in modules
  with the compiler's names (`Typing_env_level`, `Cached_level`,
  `Typing_env`). Definitions only; it extracts to plain OCaml (tried on
  the side). Its header says, field by field, what the model has of
  `Typing_env.t` and every difference.
- `FlambdaTypingEnvSpec.v`, `FlambdaTypingEnvProofs.v`: what satisfies
  an environment (`typing_env_satisfied`, `env_satisfies`) and what
  looking a name up gives. 20 statements.
- `FlambdaTypingEnvExamplesSpec.v`, `FlambdaTypingEnvExamplesProofs.v`:
  the environment that Simplify builds for a small term, the same in
  the body of a closure, a symbol, poison; with machine environments
  that do and do not satisfy each. 16 statements.

`FlambdaEnvAliases.v`, `FlambdaEnvAliasesSpec.v` and
`FlambdaEnvAliasesProofs.v` are T3's; this branch carries T3's commits
for them by cherry-pick. No stand-in for
them is committed anywhere.

- The simplifier streams: `env_satisfies tenv statics vars`, and
  `type_simple_in_term_canonical` for replacing a simple by its
  canonical element.
- What the n-way join needs of the environment (D27), from reading
  `types/env/join_env.ml`, `types/join_levels.ml` and
  `types/meet_and_n_way_join.ml` beside `types/join_levels_old.ml`:
  - Both joins read the environments at the uses through `cut` (the
    equations of the level since the fork), `find`, `mem`, `is_bottom`
    and the canonical elements, and write the result with
    `add_variable_definition ... In_types` for the variables made
    existential and with `Meet_env.add_env_extension`. The model's
    environment, levels and `cut` serve both as they are.
  - The binary join also reads the variables that the cut level
    defines (`Typing_env_level.variables_by_binding_time`,
    `find_kind`, `defined_variables_with_kinds`) and answers a level
    (`Typing_env_level.create`, in which several variables may have
    one binding time), which Simplify adds with
    `Meet_env.add_env_extension_from_level`; it uses
    `Typing_env.Join_env` (central, left and right environments, a
    depth), `compute_joined_aliases`,
    `aliases_of_simple_allowable_in_types` and
    `alias_is_bound_strictly_earlier`.
  - The n-way join reads only the equations (and the symbol
    projections) of the cut level, finds the variables by going through
    the types, and answers the environment itself. It needs, and the
    binary one does not: `Typing_env.mem` with a `min_name_mode` on the
    environment at the fork; `get_canonical_simple_exn` and
    `get_canonical_simple_ignoring_name_mode` in each environment
    joined; and `Typing_env.stable_compare_simples`, a total order on
    simples (binding time, then compilation unit, then stamp). The
    last is not in the model: it compares names by their numbers,
    which the rules for names forbid. On the variables of one
    environment it is the order of binding times, which are distinct;
    whoever models the n-way join has to say what replaces it for
    symbols and constants.
  - The n-way meet (`meet_and_n_way_join.ml`) cuts the environments of
    the two sides of a disjunction with `Typing_env.cut` after
    `Meet_env.enter_scope`; the binary meet uses `cut_as_extension`.
    Both are here.
  - Symbol projections of a level are read by both joins and are not
    in the model (lifting, S5).

## Agreed with other streams

With T3 (aliases):

- `FlambdaBindingTime.v` is T2's; T3 wrote it and does not edit it.
- The environment's map is `names_to_types : Keyed.finite_map name
  (flambda_type * Binding_time.With_name_mode.t)`, read with
  `name_eqb`, and is given to `Aliases` as it stands, as the compiler
  does. `binding_time_resolver` is a total function; the environment
  passes the one that reads the map.
- T3 exports `Aliases.t`, `Aliases.empty`, `Aliases.add resolver map t
  canonical_element1 canonical_element2 : option (Or_bottom.t
  Aliases.add_result)` (`None` is a fatal error of the compiler),
  `Aliases.get_canonical_element_exn ... : option simple` (`None` is
  `Not_found`), `Aliases.get_canonical_ignoring_name_mode`,
  `Aliases.get_aliases`, `Aliases.compute_name_mode`; and
  `aliases_respected t vars`, `aliases_consistent t`,
  `aliases_well_formed resolver map t`, `aliases_mention t n`.
- `aliases_respected (Typing_env.aliases tenv) valuation` is a conjunct
  of what it is to satisfy an environment; T2 proves that each function
  of the environment keeps it, from T3's statements.
- Used by the first request: `empty_respected`, `respected_canonical`,
  `respected_canonical_at_mode`, `canonical_at_mode_name_mode`. For the
  second: `respected_coincide`, and from T3's second request that `add`
  keeps respect when its two arguments have the same value, what its
  result is, `add_mentioned`, and that well-formedness depends on the
  map only through the binding times and modes of the names mentioned.
- T2's invariant carries "every variable that the aliases mention is
  known to the environment" (T3 cannot: a symbol with no equation has
  no entry in the map), and `aliases_consistent` rather than
  `aliases_well_formed`, which `add` does not keep for two symbols.
- A tie of binding times between two variables of the current unit is
  a fatal error in the compiler's resolver (T3's discrepancy T3-1); the
  environment's invariant (distinct binding times) makes it
  unreachable.

With T1 (grammar and meaning):

- `name_eqb` and `name_eqb_eq` are T1's; `Env_extension` holds
  `equations : Keyed.finite_map name flambda_type`.
- After the orchestrator's ruling on D26, T1 makes `relation_holds`
  say nothing of a value that is poison (`Is_int`, `Get_tag`); T2's
  exemption alone does not cover the `Is_int` and tag parameters of a
  field unboxed in turn, which hold 0.

With the orchestrator:

- D26: the exemption of poison is by the value that the valuation
  gives, for every name; the side effect on variables that exist only
  in types is in the header and has a CR. The equations of an
  extension inside a type stay as T1 has them.
