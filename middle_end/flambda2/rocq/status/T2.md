# T2, the typing environment: status

Branch `flambda2-rocq/T2-env`, on T1's `flambda2-rocq/T1-grammar`
with two commits of T3's picked on top (binding times and
name modes; the aliases as functions). Files, all in
`middle_end/flambda2/types/rocq/`:

- `FlambdaBindingTime.v`: binding times and name modes. Written by T3
  as the first commit of its branch, owned by T2.
- `FlambdaTypingEnv.v`: the environment and its functions, in modules
  with the compiler's names (`Typing_env_level`, `Cached_level`,
  `Typing_env`). Definitions only; extracts. Its header says, field by
  field, what the model has of `Typing_env.t` and every difference.
- `FlambdaTypingEnvSpec.v`, `FlambdaTypingEnvProofs.v`: what satisfies
  an environment (`typing_env_satisfied`, `env_satisfies`) and what the
  functions guarantee of it.

## Agreed with other streams

With T3 (aliases):

- `FlambdaBindingTime.v` is T2's; T3 wrote it and does not edit it.
- The environment's map is `names_to_types : Keyed.finite_map name
  (flambda_type * Binding_time.With_name_mode.t)`, read with
  `name_eqb`, and is given to `Aliases` as it stands, as the compiler
  does. `binding_time_resolver` is a total function; the environment
  passes the one that reads the map.
- T3 exports `Aliases.t`, `Aliases.empty`, `Aliases.add resolver map t
  canonical_element1 canonical_element2 : option (Or_bottom.t
  Aliases.add_result)` (`None` is a fatal error of the compiler),
  `Aliases.get_canonical_element_exn ... : option simple` (`None` is
  `Not_found`), `Aliases.get_canonical_ignoring_name_mode`,
  `Aliases.get_aliases`, `Aliases.compute_name_mode`; and, in
  `FlambdaEnvAliasesSpec.v`, `aliases_respected t vars` (names in one
  class have equal values), `aliases_consistent t`,
  `aliases_well_formed resolver map t`, `mentioned t n`.
- `aliases_respected (Typing_env.aliases tenv) valuation` is a conjunct
  of what it is to satisfy an environment; T2 proves that each function
  of the environment keeps it, from T3's statements.
- What T2 uses of T3's, by the names T3 gave: `empty_respected`,
  `respected_canonical`, `respected_canonical_at_mode` (no premise but
  respect), the name mode of what `get_canonical_element_exn` answers
  (the element itself, or of scoped mode at least `min_name_mode`),
  that `add` keeps respect when its two arguments have the same value,
  `add_mentioned`, and that respect and well-formedness depend only on
  the names mentioned.
- T2's invariant carries "every variable that the aliases mention is
  known to the environment" (T3 cannot: a symbol with no equation has
  no entry in the map), and `aliases_consistent` rather than
  `aliases_well_formed`, which `add` does not keep for two symbols.
- A tie of binding times between two variables of the current unit is
  a fatal error in the compiler's resolver (T3's discrepancy T3-1); the
  environment's invariant (distinct binding times) makes it
  unreachable.

With T1 (grammar and meaning):

- `name_eqb` and `name_eqb_eq` are T1's (`FlambdaTypeGrammar.v`,
  `FlambdaTypeGrammarProofs.v`); `Env_extension` holds
  `equations : Keyed.finite_map name flambda_type`.
