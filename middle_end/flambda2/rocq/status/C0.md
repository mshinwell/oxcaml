## How a merge goes

1. The orchestrator says "merge `<branch>` at `<commit>`, signed off by
   a reviewer". One merge at a time.
2. `git merge --ff-only`. If it does not fast-forward, say so: the
   owner rebases. There are no merge commits.
3. If the branch has a commit `<stream>: lines for the shared lists`,
   check that it touches only the lists of `rocq/Makefile` and of
   `FlambdaExtract.v`, and adds only lines for that stream's files,
   each in its place in the order (`rocq/HACKING.md`, "Adding a
   module").
4. If the branch ends with commits of a test tool or of examples
   (what needs the regenerated extracted files), do not fast-forward
   those: fast-forward to the commit before them, then apply them in
   order, the first as one commit with the regenerated files
   (`rocq/HACKING.md`, "Who commits the extracted files" and the item
   after it). The compiler must build at every commit of this branch.
5. On top, as commits of C0's: the regenerated extracted files where
   step 4 did not make them, and whatever of `rocq/HACKING.md` and of
   the layout of `OWNERS.md` the merge calls for.
6. Validate (below) and report the new tip to the orchestrator.

A series goes back to its owner, whatever else is right in it, if a
document or a comment of it cites a commit that is not on this branch
or a file that is not in the tree (`rocq/HACKING.md`, "The semantics of
Flambda 2"). A label of a decision or of a discrepancy that a merge
brings and `DECISIONS.md` has not got is given its line there, in a
commit of C0's on top.

## How an epoch goes

Agreed with the orchestrator for C1, and the same for the others.

1. The owner of the change puts its series on the integration tip, on a
   new branch, in four parts: (a) moves of text and nothing else; (b)
   the change, with its repairs in every file, its lines of the lists
   and the text of the ledger that the reviewer signed off; (c) its
   note, its status and its section of `OWNERS.md`; (d) last, the test
   tool and the examples. Each commit of (a) to (c) passes
   `make -C rocq all` and `make -C rocq assumptions`.
2. The reviewer confirms that the series is the text reviewed.
3. C0 stages it on a branch of its own, not on the integration branch:
   fast-forward (a) to (c); one commit of (d) with the regenerated
   extracted files; one commit for `rocq/HACKING.md`, `OWNERS.md` and
   this file. Then the whole validation, with `make -s test`.
4. Only then is the integration branch fast-forwarded to the staged
   tip, validated again, and the new tip reported. Nothing else is
   merged from step 1 to that report.

## Decided by the orchestrator

- `FlambdaControlFlow*`, `FlambdaNominal*`, `FlambdaAlpha*`,
  `FlambdaApply*` and `FlambdaStatic*` are C0's until S3, N1, S4 and S5
  are staffed, and then go to them as `OWNERS.md` says.
- The converter moves to a library after the second epoch (it was to
  stay in `flsemantics.ml` until V2 and V3 were staffed; T7 needs it).
- What needs the regenerated extracted files may be a short series at
  the end of a branch; C0 puts the regenerated files in the first.
- The order of the series that change the machine, after the fifth
  epoch: the first step of the heap, then C2's tests of kinds at
  binders, then C3's partial applications and over-applications,
  which is merged as an epoch; V3's later series, V4's passes on
  loops and V2's wiring are replayed over them by their owners.
  Between two of these that are ready together the order is as
  listed; one that is ready is not held for one that is not.
- A statement whose text does not change and whose meaning does,
  because a definition that it mentions changes, is treated as a
  changed statement: it needs the word of the owner of its file and
  of the reviewer that signed it.
- For a field added to a record of the model, the meaning of a
  statement changes only where something that it mentions reads the
  field. The reviewer of the commit that adds the field records
  three things, checked mechanically: which definitions read the
  field (a search of the tree for the field and its projections,
  and the build), every other definition being parametric in it;
  that a test of equality on the record still answers true exactly
  on equal records, so that every statement that goes through a
  comparison of terms says of the same relation what it said; and
  that no statement of a `Spec` file changes in text and every
  proof passes. The comparison is then stricter pair by pair, and
  nothing changes on terms in which the field is nowhere set; that
  no verdict of the validation moves is measured by the author, on
  the sets that it names, and not proved, because rewrites of the
  validation use the comparison as a test too, and the ledger says
  so. With that, the reviewers of the other `Spec` files are
  not asked for a line each; a reviewer is asked only if a file
  that it signed has a definition that reads the field. The owners
  of the files touched give their words as always.
- The hour of the sweep that put the heap into the state was given
  by the orchestrator, with notice to the human, when the stack
  with V4's placing of continuations had landed; nothing landed
  between the two. It went in on the build of every proof, the
  audit of the branch and the validation at the exact commit, and
  not on a reviewer's reading of each changed statement.
- With imports declared, the two runs of a unit are defined and
  compared, and `validate_unit_sound` is about runs from the empty
  static environment, where a unit that reads an import is
  undefined. Until the theorem is stated and proved for runs from
  the static environment of the imports, and R1 has signed that
  statement: no commit of the tool is merged that prints a bare
  "validated" for a unit that mentions an import (it prints the
  line that the validation says nothing of the unit, as for a term
  before that is undefined); no table counts such a unit as
  validated; and the trust ledger says that the comparison of the
  runs with imports is a test. C0 checks it where the commit of
  the tool is picked and at each commit of tables after it.
