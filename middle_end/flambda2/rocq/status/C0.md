## How a merge goes

1. The orchestrator says "merge `<branch>` at `<commit>`, signed off by
   a reviewer". One merge at a time.
2. `git merge --ff-only`. If it does not fast-forward, say so: the
   owner rebases. There are no merge commits.
3. If the branch has a commit `<stream>: lines for the shared lists`,
   check that it touches only the lists of `rocq/Makefile` and of
   `FlambdaExtract.v`, and adds only lines for that stream's files,
   each in its place in the order (`rocq/HACKING.md`, "Adding a
   module").
4. If the branch ends with a commit of the test tool or of examples
   (what needs the regenerated extracted files), do not fast-forward
   that one: fast-forward to the commit before it, then make one commit
   of it and of the regenerated files (`rocq/HACKING.md`, "Who commits
   the extracted files" and the item after it). The compiler must build
   at every commit of this branch.
5. On top, as commits of C0's: the regenerated extracted files where
   step 4 did not make them, and whatever of `rocq/HACKING.md` and of
   the layout of `OWNERS.md` the merge calls for.
6. Validate (below) and report the new tip to the orchestrator.

## How an epoch goes

Agreed with the orchestrator for C1, and the same for the others.

1. The owner of the change puts its series on the integration tip, on a
   new branch, in four parts: (a) moves of text and nothing else; (b)
   the change, with its repairs in every file, its lines of the lists
   and the text of the ledger that the reviewer signed off; (c) its
   note, its status and its section of `OWNERS.md`; (d) last, the test
   tool and the examples. Each commit of (a) to (c) passes
   `make -C rocq all` and `make -C rocq assumptions`.
2. The reviewer confirms that the series is the text reviewed.
3. C0 stages it on a branch of its own, not on the integration branch:
   fast-forward (a) to (c); one commit of (d) with the regenerated
   extracted files; one commit for `rocq/HACKING.md`, `OWNERS.md` and
   this file. Then the whole validation, with `make -s test`.
4. Only then is the integration branch fast-forwarded to the staged
   tip, validated again, and the new tip reported. Nothing else is
   merged from step 1 to that report.
