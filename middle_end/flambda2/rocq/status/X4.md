# X4, access to the compiler's types for tests: status

Branch `flambda2-rocq/X4-test-access`, cut from the integration branch
at 0a2933e7c9 and rebased onto it since. One of the two streams
authorised to change production code (decision D33). The design, with
the options, the questions for the human and what building it showed,
is `middle_end/flambda2/rocq/requests/X4-test-access.md`.

## The task

- A. Accessors for `Type_grammar.Block_size` (settled by the human).
- B. A way for tests to read the types Simplify gives, with
  `Flambda2_types.t` kept abstract.
- C. A hook in Simplify for the types it had.

Each change to the compiler is a commit of its own, with a subject in
the compiler's style, touching only compiler files and its test. The
documents are one commit, last.

## Decided overnight (2026-11-11, the human away; for his review)

By the orchestrator, on the human's instruction to decide and go on:

- B and C go ahead as option R of the note: an observer registered
  through the internal `Typing_env`, one public function
  `Typing_env.notify_test_observer`, called by Simplify at the start
  of each continuation handler and at the end of the unit.
- The five openings of binders that do not freshen
  (`pattern_match_for_printing` in `terms/flambda.mli`) are exported,
  for tests (N1 of the note).
- Conditions, all met: each is its own commit with a test; nothing
  changes for ordinary clients; each new entry point has a
  `CR mshinwell` saying it is for tests and was decided in his
  absence; the testsuite passes; R4 reviews; C0 takes the commits.

By me, within that:

- The names, and the reference kept in `typing_env.ml` (the note's
  question 2): `Typing_env.For_testing.set_observer` (internal),
  `Typing_env.notify_test_observer` (public).
- No flag around the two calls in Simplify (question 3): the cost with
  no observer is one test of a reference at each continuation handler
  and once for each unit.
- The openings are named `pattern_match_for_printing`, as the function
  of `Name_abstraction` that they call, and so freshen when `-dfreshen`
  is given.
- The accessors are the identity and check nothing; the interface says
  so (asked by R4).
- "After each `Let`" and "at the entry of each function body" are not
  reported: T7's first version does not need them.

To undo: revert the commit of the observer, or that of the openings;
each stands alone (the test of the observer loses its last two lines
of output with the openings).

## Done

The three compiler commits, in the order of the branch:

1. `flambda2: Add accessors for Type_grammar.Block_size`.
   `Block_size.of_target_ocaml_int : Target_ocaml_int.t -> t` and
   `to_target_ocaml_int : t -> Target_ocaml_int.t`, in
   `types/grammar/type_grammar.{ml,mli}`. T7 confirmed that these two
   are all its converter needs (they replace its `int_of_block_size`
   and `block_size_of_int`).
2. `flambda2: Let tests observe the typing environments of Simplify`.
   `types/env/typing_env.{ml,mli}`, `types/flambda2_types.mli`,
   `simplify/simplify_let_cont_expr.ml` (start of `simplify_handler`),
   `simplify/simplify.ml` (`run`, with the final environment and the
   return continuation of the unit). Test:
   `tests/api_tests/typing_env_observer.{ml,fl,expected}` and a stanza
   in `tests/api_tests/dune`.
3. `flambda2: Export the openings of binders that do not freshen
   names`. `terms/flambda.{ml,mli}`: `pattern_match_for_printing` in
   `Let_expr`, `Continuation_handler`,
   `Non_recursive_let_cont_handler`, `Recursive_let_cont_handlers` and
   `Function_params_and_body`, each with the type of the
   `pattern_match` beside it. The test of the observer gains two lines:
   the output opened without freshening has the continuation that was
   reported; opened with freshening it has not.

And the design note, with section 7 on what building them showed: no
continuation reported twice on the examples of `tests/semantics`;
every handler of the outputs was reported; none is found when the
output is opened with freshening; parameters made on the way up are
not in the environment reported; a stored name can be bound in two
places (discrepancy X4-1); the `.cmx` files of the standard library
are read through the same public function.

## Agreed with other streams

T7 (differential tests):

- A: the two functions above, nothing more (no `print`, no `compare`).
- B: a callback registered through an internal module of the library,
  which receives the internal `Typing_env.t`, is what it wants; it
  already uses `Flambda2_types__Typing_env`, whose `find` gives a
  `Type_grammar.t`. It will run Simplify in its own process, by
  `Flambda2.flambda_to_flambda` on a parsed `.fl` file, as
  `tests/tools/flsemantics.ml` does.
- C, first version: the environment at the entry of each continuation
  handler and the final environment of the unit; identification by
  the continuation. Not needed yet: after each `Let`. The call must
  not change what Simplify does. Names that hold poison are exempted
  by the test (D26); the hook filters nothing.
- Told to T7: reports arrive for handlers that are not in the output
  (inlined at a single use, first pass before specialisation, rejected
  inlining), to be matched by continuation and counted; parameters
  made on the way up are not in the environment (`mem` before `find`);
  the callback must only read; the join of the arguments is the type
  of a parameter only with `-flambda2-join-points`; any continuation
  reported twice is to be told to X4.
- On the names: T7 confirms that every opening of a binder that the
  terms export freshens, that `flsemantics.ml` opens all five
  constructs so, and that the Rocq machine is lexically scoped at a
  handler entry. It asked for the openings with the signatures of the
  `pattern_match` beside them, so that the term converter (C0's) takes
  the openings as a parameter. Its converter checks, at each binder
  opened without freshening, that the name is not already in scope,
  and reports a unit where that fails.

X3 (physical equality; the other production series): X3 touches
`driver/oxcaml_{flags,args}`, `ui/flambda_features`,
`types/grammar/type_grammar.{ml,mli}` (a structural `equal`, after
`project_variables_out` and beside `val kind`), `types/env/join_env.ml`,
`types/meet_and_n_way_join.ml`, `types/meet_and_join.ml`, and
`tests/api_tests/{dune,join_sharing.ml,join_sharing.expected}`. X4
touches `type_grammar.{ml,mli}` in the `Block_size` submodule only,
`types/env/typing_env.{ml,mli}`, `types/flambda2_types.mli`,
`simplify/simplify_let_cont_expr.ml`, `simplify/simplify.ml`,
`terms/flambda.{ml,mli}` and `tests/api_tests/`. Shared files:
`type_grammar.{ml,mli}` and `tests/api_tests/dune`, in different
places (both series add stanzas at the end of that `dune` file).

R4 (reviewer): the accessors read as exact identities; asked that the
interface say they are unchecked. For the observer: asked that both
interfaces say that the final environment of a unit is reported under
its return continuation, and that a report need not be one handler of
the output. Both done.
