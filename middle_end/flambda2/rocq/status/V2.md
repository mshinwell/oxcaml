# V2: the corpus, and units made from OCaml sources

Branch `flambda2-rocq/V2-examples`.

## Done

- **A path from an OCaml source to the validator.**
  `middle_end/flambda2/tests/semantics/ocaml/from_ocaml.sh` makes of
  `X.ml` the term that `-drawfexpr` prints (`-nopervasives -nostdlib
  -stop-after middle_end`, with `X.mli` if there is one); the test tool
  reads it. The dune rules of that directory make each term again,
  compare it with the committed `X.fl`, run the tool and compare with
  `X.expected`. Why both the source and the term are committed: the
  term can be read without building, and the rule shows that it is what
  the compiler prints today. `README.md` there has the command, what
  each verdict means and the table of examples.
- **The conversion drops depth variables** (a hack the human asked
  for, with CRs): every function begins with a binding of a `Rec_info`.
- **`load_known_blocks`** (`FlambdaBlockLoadSpec.v`, proved to refine):
  `Block_load`, `Is_int` and `Get_tag` of a variable bound to a
  `Make_block` or of a symbol bound to a static block. Every unit from
  OCaml needs it for its module block. Wired into `normalise_before`.
- **`remove_unused_conts`** (`FlambdaUnusedContsSpec.v`, proved to be
  an equivalence). Wired into `normalise_before`.
- **Sixteen OCaml examples**: eight validated, seven "the same" and not
  validated, one not checked. `get_tag.fl` of the old examples is now
  validated too.
- `requests/V2-missing-constructs.md`: what the model and the validator
  are missing, ranked, with the fragments.

Fifty-two probes of one feature each were also run. After the two
rewrites: the units that use only integer arithmetic,
comparisons other than equality, tuples, variants, records, boxed
integers and non-recursive functions convert and run the same; those
that stop are in the request file, by construct. Three are undefined
before Simplify in the model though they are defined programs (a
tupled function, a partial application and an over-application through
an unknown function): the request file, section 2.

Nothing was found where the two runs differ, and nothing where the
validator accepts a pair it should not.

## What "validated" rests on, for a unit from OCaml

Beyond the trust ledger (the conversion to the model, the parser):

- the printing of `-drawfexpr`: the term validated is the one the
  parser reads back. It is the term the compiler simplifies only as far
  as printing and parsing lose nothing, and they do lose something: the
  kinds of the fields of a `Make_block` (read back as `any_value`) and
  the arities of calls (discrepancies V2-2, C2-5, R2-12);
- that the tool runs Simplify as the compiler would: the tool uses the
  default flags, and so does the command that makes the term;
- the depth variables and coercions dropped by the conversion.

Proposed line for the ledger, for a reviewer to sign:

> **The printing of a unit from an OCaml source**, by `-drawfexpr`
> (`parser/flambda_to_fexpr.ml`, `parser/print_fexpr.ml`), for the
> examples of `middle_end/flambda2/tests/semantics/ocaml/`. Trusted:
> that the term the parser reads back from what is printed is the term
> the compiler gave to Simplify. It is known not to be, in the kinds of
> the fields of blocks and in the arities of calls; what is validated
> is the term read back. It goes when the tool takes the unit from the
> compiler directly and not through the printer.
