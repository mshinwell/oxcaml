# T7, differential tests of types and the converter: status

Branch `flambda2-rocq/T7-difftest`. Everything of mine is in
`middle_end/flambda2/tests/semantics/types/`:

- `model_numbers.ml`, `model_names.ml`, `type_converter.ml`: the
  converter between `Type_grammar.t` and the model's `flambda_type`,
  both ways, with the numbers and the numbering of names. Trusted,
  unverified. Library `flambda2_rocq_types_converter`.
- `model_type.ml` (same library): when two types of the model are the
  same (up to how maps and sets are written), and their printer. Part
  of what the tests are trusted for, not of the conversion.
- `type_generator.ml`: a seeded generator of types of the model, raw
  or keeping what the compiler's creation functions keep, and a
  shrinker.
- `type_comparisons.ml`: how a comparison is run and reported, and
  the comparisons of one type, for both programs below.
- `types_difftest.ml`, `types_difftest.expected`: the comparisons on
  generated and chosen types. Under the `runtest` alias of the
  semantics tests; a few seconds.
- `types_of_compilations.ml`, with `types_of_compilations.units` and
  `types_of_compilations.expected`: the comparisons of one type on
  the types that Simplify computes for the units listed. Under the
  same alias; half a minute.

This file cites no commit: the commits of a series get other hashes
when it is applied.

## The series

The first series is on the integration branch: who owns the tests,
the lines for the shared lists, the converter, the generator and the
comparisons, and seventeen commits of tests and repairs, with the two
items of the trust ledger. What its commits did, and what the epochs
and the reviews changed in it, is in the history of this file.

The second series, on that, each commit building with the test's
output equal to its expected file:

1. two answers printed are compared up to where their lines are
   broken (`same_text`);
2. examples of the join of types, written down by the owner of the
   join's transcription, run in the compiler;
3. the equality of types and the predicate of well-formed types, on
   the compiler's types;
4. the comparisons of one type are in a module of their own (text
   moved; no output changes);
5. the types that Simplify computes, against the model;
6. the status;
7. what the converter drops of `Rec_info` is put back, and the types
   are compared whole (after review);
8. the examples of the join say where each is from, and what was
   compared is counted (after review);
9. this status.

It changes no compiler code and no `.v` file; it uses the observer
of typing environments that the types library now has for tests.
Commits 7 and 8 repair what the reviewer found in 2 and 5: a textual
test of what differs in a type that comes back, a free variable
forgiven by its kind, a round trip that compared a type with itself,
two examples whose inputs were not those of the file, and counts
that put together what was compared and what was only shown.

With the owner of the grammar and meaning of types:

- `Env_extension` holds a `Keyed.finite_map name flambda_type`, the
  known tags a `Keyed.finite_map Z`. `type_satisfied_b` is a bare
  boolean with its parts callable on their own.
- Told to that owner, for its header: what the converter drops (the
  `rec_info` of a function type, the expression of a `Rec_info` head,
  the string of `Poison`), what it refuses, how numbers are read.
- `free_variables` of a type with a shadowed binding lists the
  variables of the shadowed binding; the compiler has no such binding.
  The comparison is exact on types without shadowed bindings and an
  inclusion otherwise.
- `these_naked_immediates` and `these_tagged_immediates` on a list
  with a repeat give a head with the set, where the compiler, given
  the set, gives the alias to the constant. The two mean the same.

With the owner of the typing environment: a valuation may give no
value to a variable that the environment knows, and nothing is then
asked of it; the obligations of meet and join are stated with no name
exempt. My check of a join follows this.

Rulings I work under: the n-way join is the one to model and the
binary join is an oracle to compare with (D27); a name holding poison
is exempt when types are checked against a run, and counted (D26);
physical equality is not modelled, and the compiler is run with the
structural setting of the flag that removes the dependence (D30);
where the model is coarser than the compiler's equality of types
(`rec_info`, the expression of a `Rec_info` head, the string of
`Poison`, a relation bound to no name), the correspondence is stated
only on types that do not differ there, and such differences are
counted apart.

## Done, with the counts of the expected file

- The converter, both directions, constructor for constructor. It
  reaches the compiler's modules as `Flambda2_types__...`, with no
  change to the compiler. `Type_grammar.Block_size.t`, abstract even
  there, is read with `Row_like_for_blocks.get_singleton` and made
  with `Row_like_for_blocks.create`.
- No function of the model disagrees with the compiler's: the round
  trips; `kind`; `get_alias_opt`; `is_obviously_bottom`;
  `is_obviously_unknown`; `free_variables` against `free_names`; 28
  creation functions (6000 calls); `Relation.of_const`;
  `Row_like_for_blocks.is_bottom`. The ranges of numbers: 52
  constants, the four ends of each of the thirteen kinds of number,
  are in the range of the model (`const_in_range`) exactly when the
  converter accepts them, and those come back unchanged; 12 constants
  made in the compiler with the top bit set (the floats that are

  come back unchanged.
- The two implementations of meet and join, each called by its own
  module (`Meet_and_join` with `Join_levels_old`;
  `Meet_and_n_way_join` with `Join_env`): the meets give the same
  answer on 1000 pairs; the joins differ on 209 (T7-3).
- Against the meaning (`type_satisfied_b`), in generated worlds:
  - a value of one of two types has their join: every case that ran,
    both implementations, at every kind (before the repair of T1-4 in

  - a value of both types has their meet, and what the meet teaches is
    true: every case that ran, but for the reduction of `Get_tag`
    (T1-1), at naked immediates and, with the relation in the
    immediates of a variant, at kind value;
  - a value of the meet has both types: fails on losses of precision

- Chosen joins: two blocks (0, 0) and (1, 1) (the binary join gives
  each field `{0, 1}`, the n-way join one variable of its own for

  R1-15 (the n-way join drops an extension that the binary join

- The comparison by text renames the variables that are not of the
