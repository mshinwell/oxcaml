# V4: loops

Branch `flambda2-rocq/V4-loops`. The aim: units made from OCaml sources
that have a loop (a `while`, a `for`, a tail-recursive function) are
validated by `FlambdaValidateSpec.validate_unit`, and not only found
the same on one run.

## Done

- **`rewrite_rec_handlers`** (`FlambdaRecHandlersSpec.v`, proved): a
  rewrite that refines, or is an equivalence, under all environments
  may be applied to the handlers of recursive continuations. It is what
  lets the existing passes into a loop; they need no change. Not yet
  wired into `normalise_before` (see "In progress").

## What `while_loop.ml` needs

From the two normalised terms that the test tool prints
(`FLSEMANTICS_PRINT_TERMS`), the term before differs from the term
after in this, and in nothing else:

| Where | The term before has | The term after has | What is missing |
|-------|---------------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| in the loop | `Untag_immediate` of a variable bound to `Tag_immediate` of the test | the test | no pass goes into a recursive handler (V2's untagging pass, once there, does the rest) |
| in the loop | a continuation applied once, from the handler of the next `Let_cont` | the handler in place | `inline_tail_conts` wants the application at the end of the body of the `Let_cont` itself |
| in the loop | `cont k (0, total, i)` in an arm of the `Switch` | `cont k (total)` | the two arguments are for parameters that are not used |
| after the loop | `k (unit, total, i) = cont k' (total)` | `k (total) = ...` | the same, at the binder |
| after the loop | a chain of three continuations each applied once from the handler of the next, with a `Let` between two of them | one handler | as the second line |

## In progress

- `sink_let_conts`: an equivalence that moves a non-recursive
  `Let_cont` to the handler that alone uses it, across `Let`s and other
  `Let_cont`s, so that `inline_tail_conts` fires.
- `drop_unused_params`: a refinement that removes the parameters of a
  continuation that its handler does not use, at the binder and at
  every application in scope, in recursive handlers too.
- The wiring into `normalise_before` and `normalise_after`, agreed with
  V2 and V3, who edit the same function.
- Loopify, for tail-recursive functions.
