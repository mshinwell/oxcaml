## Decided overnight

Each is a choice the human may reverse; the cost of doing so is given.

1. **A named predicate for a rewrite that takes only a term**
   (`rewrite_equivariant f`), where N1's two statements are written
   out. Options: write each out; a predicate. Chosen: the predicate,
   since `rewrite_code` and `iterate` take a rewrite as an argument and
   their statements need it anyway. It unfolds to N1's form. The
   reviewer agreed. Cost of changing: one line a statement.
2. **`expr_in_scope` and `inlinable_b` are stated here**, from N1's
   lemmas in `FlambdaEquivarianceProofs.v`, so that every check of
   `FlambdaNormaliseSpec.v` has a statement. The reviewer agreed. Cost
   of changing: delete two statements.
3. **The action on what a pass carries is defined in my `Spec` file**
   (`rename_known_blocks`, `rename_known_numbers`,
   `rename_naive_typing_env`), not in the file of the pass, since I do
   not edit those. They are definitions to review. The one for the
   typing environment is named "naive" so as not to take the name that
   the action on the types proper will want. Cost of changing: move
   three definitions to their owners' files.
4. **`follow_aliases` is stated and proved here**, though the file is
   of the types library. Its owner was asked and had not answered
   after twenty minutes; `validate_unit` needs it. Cost of changing:
   move two statements and 250 lines of proof.
5. **`static_names` is stated with a premise** on the function it is
   given (that it reads no variable and no continuation), and again
   for the two functions used. Options: only the two instances.
   Chosen: both. Cost of changing: delete one statement.
6. **`envs_free_of` and `static_fault` are included**, which are tests
   on environments and states and not checks that the validator runs:
   they are what two statements of their files are about, and the
   action on states was there. Cost of changing: delete two
   statements.
7. **`validate_unit` has no statement yet**, on N1's instruction: the
   statement about the comparison is on a series this branch does not
   sit on. Options: (a) wait; (b) prove the fact about the comparison
   a second time here; (c) a statement with a premise. Chosen: (a),
   with the proof done under a hypothesis so that the statement is one
   line at the rebase. Cost: none; (b) would be about a hundred lines
   to delete later.

Nothing was found that is not equivariant as it stands: no rewrite or
check in the base makes a name or goes by the order of names. The one
place where names are made from others is `inline_renaming`, which is
made of swaps (N1's proof). No discrepancy with the compiler was found.
