# T1, grammar and meaning of Flambda types: status

Branch `flambda2-rocq/T1-grammar`, on the integration branch after the
first epoch (primitives as data) and its three leaves. Files, all in
`middle_end/flambda2/types/rocq/`:

- `FlambdaTypeGrammar.v`: the grammar (`flambda_type` and the types
  defined with it), `kind`, and the creation functions of
  `type_grammar.ml` and `more_type_creators.ml` that the statements
  use. Definitions only; reviewed against the OCaml like
  `FlambdaModel.v`. Its header says, constructor by constructor, what
  the model has.
- `FlambdaTypeGrammarProofs.v`: `types_induction`, and `name_eqb_eq`.
- `FlambdaTypeMeaningSpec.v`: `type_satisfied statics vars v t`, what it
  reads of a value, and the statements.
- `FlambdaTypeMeaningProofs.v`: the proofs, one equation for each
  constructor (hint database `type_satisfied`), and the examples.
- `FlambdaTypeMeaningBoolSpec.v`: `type_satisfied_b` and the tests of
  the other functions of the meaning, with the statements that each
  answers `true` exactly when the meaning holds.
- `FlambdaTypeMeaningBoolProofs.v`: their proofs.

Today's `FlambdaTypesSpec.v` and `FlambdaTypesProofs.v` are untouched
and still build; `FlambdaAliases*` and the validator use them.

## Where the review stands

- R1 and R3 read the grammar and the meaning. R3 signed off the
  branch as it stood after "T1: lines for the shared lists" over the
  first epoch, for the stated restricted denotation: the move to
  `Keyed`, the rebase, the boolean meaning, the ruling on poison and
  the comments. One commit of wording followed, at its request (the
  boolean `boxable_number`; `Is_null` and poison of kind value).
- R3-15, not blocking: a constant out of range is an immediate for the
  types and the primitives have no value on it. Closed when "constants
  are in range" is a condition of well-formed terms (D32, D13); the
  Spec header says so.

## Done

First merge request (with R1): the grammar and the denotation. On the
integration branch it is 4100198eb1.

- The grammar, in full but for `Array` (needs subkinds; the machine has
  no arrays), the `rec_info` of a function type and the head of a
  `Rec_info` type (the terms have no `Rec_info`).
- `type_satisfied`, a structural `Fixpoint` on the type, so it unfolds
  and computes. Maps inside types are read through `lookup` only.
- 35 statements, all proved: what the map combinators mean; the kind of
  a value that has a type; `Unknown`, `Bottom`, aliases; one equivalence
  for each creation function (`null`, `any_non_null_value`,
  `create_variant`, `tag_immediate`, `these_tagged_immediates`,
  `any_block`, `immutable_block`, `box_number`,
  `this_immutable_string`, `exactly_this_closure`, the three relation
  types, ...); the row-like structures, the closures entry and the
  environment extension by `lookup`.
- Examples for every constructor: a value that has a type and one that
  has not (module `Examples` of the Proofs file).
- Validation of it then: `make -C rocq -j16 all` and
  `make -C rocq assumptions` pass (174 axioms, those of the ledger; the
  Flambda 2 statements depend on the 7 of its section and no other);
  the semantics tests pass; nothing extracted changes.

Second merge request, on top: what T2 to T7 need.

- Validation of the second request (the commit before the last "lines

After R1's review of the first request (changes required, two blocking
findings, both about the relations), on top of the second request:

- B1, `Get_tag`. A relation of a type is not the execution of its
  primitive. `relation_holds` for `Get_tag` now also relates a tagged
  immediate to any number: unboxing records the relation of a parameter
  that may be an immediate and passes the tag 0 there. The primitive
  stays undefined on an immediate.
- B2, `Is_null`. The two directions disagreed on poison and on a symbol
  that is not bound. `relation_holds` for `Is_null` now relates every
  value of kind `Value` (1 for `Null`, 0 for any other), which is the
  domain the type of the scrutinee admits and the compiler's reading of
  a constant.
- `Is_int` checked for the same: its two directions agree (tagged
  immediates and blocks), and are unchanged.
- `Relation.of_const`, the compiler's reading of a relation on a
  constant, is in the grammar, with `relation_of_const_sound` (where
  the relation holds of a constant and the compiler gives a number, it
  is that number: what T4 needs for
  `reduce_head_of_kind_naked_immediate`) and `is_null_of_const` (for
  `Is_null` it is exact at kind value). For `Is_int` the compiler's
  reading is wider than the relation, and its two creation paths
  disagree on `Null` (discrepancy T1-2).
- New statements `is_null_coherent`, `is_int_coherent`,
  `get_tag_coherent`: the variable of a type holds a number that the
  relation relates to each value of the type. The three statements for
  the relation types spell the relation out.
- `relation_get_tag` is weaker: the value is an immediate or a block
  with one of the tags. So one reduction of the compiler's `Meet`
  (`reduce_inverse_relations`, the `Get_tag` case) is not sound for
  this meaning: it makes the value a block. For T4 and the human, see
  the open questions.
- Examples: the unboxing case; `Is_null` of poison and of an unbound
  symbol, both sides; mixed and float shapes, with rejection by shape
  from an inhabited type; `closure_types`; closures with at least some
  contents (`at_least_this_closure`, with its statement); a second kind
  of naked number and of boxed number. The claim is now "for each
  constructor but `Mutable_block`".
- Found while checking the unboxing case: unboxing gives types to
  parameters that hold poison (a field, unboxed in turn, of a variant
  that is an immediate on that path). They were not true for the
  meaning here, where a poison value has no type that says what it is.
  Examples `unboxed_poison_*`; discrepancy T1-3. Decided since (D26),
  see "After the first epoch".
- Discrepancies recorded (`middle_end/flambda2/rocq/DECISIONS.md` has
  their titles): T1-1 (`Get_tag` in `reduce_inverse_relations`, with a
  run of the compiler's types library), T1-2 (`Is_int` of a constant),
  T1-3 (types of poison parameters in unboxing).

After R3's review of the second request (changes required, one
blocking finding and three notes), on top of the above:

- Blocking: a function type of a set of closures made the function
  exist. `closures_entry_satisfied` now says the code of a function
  only if the set has the function, as the closure types and the value
  slot types already did; what the set has is for the index. New
  statements `closures_case_function_code` (the code is known where
  the index has the slot) and `closures_case_own_code` (and always for
  the slot of the closure itself, with `closure_in_set_slot`): the two
  cases in which a prover reads a function type.
- The compiler has two implementations of meet and join. The default,
  at every optimisation level, is `meet_and_join.ml` (binary join);
  `meet_and_n_way_join.ml` runs only with the n-way join algorithm or
  match-in-match. The reviews and the plan for T4 cite the second. The
  two joins differ on function types (discrepancy T1-4): the n-way one
  keeps a function type that one input has, and is then not an upper
  bound where another input says nothing of the code of the closure's
  own function (`meet_code_ids` answers the code; reproduced with the
  types library; examples `nway_join_*`).
- Notes 1 and 2 (code age, allocation modes and uniqueness): the
  header says what the restriction means for T4, T6 and S4.
- Note 3 (what meet relies on in raw structures): the header lists the
  conditions (product no wider than the index, slots among those of
  the index, shapes written in one way, kinds), and four statements
  say what each gives for one case: `block_case_empty`,
  `closures_case_function_code`, `closures_case_value_slot`,
  `block_shapes_disjoint`. The predicate over a whole type, with the
  creation functions establishing it, is not written yet (see
  "Next").
- The header says what a meet shortcut of the provers answers about
  (not every value of the type).
- Discrepancies recorded: T1-4 (the two joins), T1-5 (what the
  creation functions and raw constructors leave unchecked, and a
  comment on products that the code contradicts).

The move to `Keyed` (C0's maps for any key type), reviewed by R3:

- The maps of types are `Keyed` maps: `known_tags` is
  `Keyed.finite_map Z`, `equations` of an environment extension is
  `Keyed.finite_map name` (first binding wins), with `name_eqb` and its
  proof `name_eqb_eq`. My own combinators are gone (`every`,
  `for_all_bindings`, `at_binding`, `at_tag`, `find_tag`,
  `finite_name_map`, `lookup_name`, `for_all_names`).
- Wording: what is said of poison, and that the creation functions
  give well-formed types "if their callers give them what they
  expect".

## After the first epoch

- Rebased. The meaning reads a value with the machine's observations
  (`kind_of_value`, `block_of`, `boxed_number_of`, `string_of`, in
  `FlambdaValueSpec.v`); my copies and my `observations_disjoint` are
  deleted for the machine's, which have the same names and types. My
  boolean `boxable_number` (a test on naked number kinds) is deleted
  with them and has no counterpart of that type: the machine's
  `boxable_number` is a datatype, and `boxable_number_of_kind` keeps
  the classification. No statement changed its text for that. The
  proposition that a value is a tagged immediate is renamed
  `is_immediate` (the machine has a boolean `is_tagged_immediate`, of
  the immediates in range).
- New statement `primitive_related`: the result of `Is_null`, of
  `Is_int` with `variant_only` and of `Get_tag`, where the primitive
  has one, is related to its argument by `relation_holds`. This is all
  that ties the relations to the primitives; the converse is false by
  design (`Get_tag` on immediates, `Is_null` on poison and unbound
  symbols, both on poison).
- `type_satisfied_b` and the tests of the other functions of the
  meaning (`relation_holds_b`, `head_of_kind_value_satisfied_b`, ...,
  `env_extension_satisfied_b`, `row_like_block_case_satisfied_b`,
  `row_like_closures_case_satisfied_b`), each with a statement
  `..._b_iff_statement`. Plain booleans, no rank 2; they are meant to
  be extracted by T7. `value_eqb`, the equality test of values that
  the test of an alias needs, is defined there and is provisional: it
  should be the machine's (asked of C0).
- D26, with the ruling on relations: a relation says nothing of a
  value that is poison. `relation_holds` for `Is_int` and `Get_tag`
  relates poison to every number; `is_poison` is new. Changed
  statements: `is_int_for_scrutinee_satisfied`,
  `get_tag_for_block_satisfied`, `relation_is_int`,
  `relation_get_tag` (each gains the case of poison),
  `relation_of_const_sound` (excepts `Is_int` of a poison constant).
  Unchanged: the coherence statements, `is_null_of_const`,
  `relation_is_null`, `primitive_related`, and the types of a poison
  value. Examples: `unboxed_poison_is_int_has` and
  `unboxed_poison_tag_has` changed sides; `unboxed_poison_has_not`
  stays; two new ones.
- `CR mshinwell` comments in the Spec, as the orchestrator asked: at
  `relation_holds` (poison under `Is_int` and `Get_tag`, D26; poison
  under `Is_null`, D8), at `type_satisfied` (the types of a poison
  value, D26), at `relation_get_tag_statement` (less than
  `reduce_inverse_relations` concludes; T1-1, R3-2), at
  `closures_entry_satisfied` (code age left out).
- Validation at the tip: `make -C rocq -j16 all` (205 claims, 68 of
  them here), `make -C rocq assumptions`, the semantics tests; nothing
  extracted changes.

## After the second epoch

Rebased on the integration branch after the epoch of sets of closures
(C3). What changed in the reviewed text, and nothing else did:

- `unnamed_function_slot`, `closure_in_set`, `project_function_slot`
  and `closure_in_set_slot_statement` are gone from
  `FlambdaTypeMeaningSpec.v`, and `function_slot` from
  `FlambdaTypeGrammar.v`. The meaning reads a closure through the
  machine's `closure_in_set` and `project_function_slot`
  (`FlambdaValueSpec.v`), which have the names and the types that the
  stand-ins had; the text of `type_satisfied` is the same. A `Closures`
  type is now about the closures of sets of several functions too,
  made by a `Let` (`Value_closure_of_set`) and static
  (`Static_closure_of_set`): the function types of an entry are about
  the code of every function slot of the set, and its closure types
  about what `project_function_slot` gives, which from a symbol is the
  symbol that the set names for the slot.
- `value_eqb` (`FlambdaTypeMeaningBoolSpec.v`) has the case of
  `Value_closure_of_set`.
- In the proofs: `closure_in_set_slot` is the machine's
  (`FlambdaSemanticsProofs.v`); the lemmas by cases on values and
  `value_induction` have the sixth case.
- Seven examples of a set of two functions, made by a `Let` and
  static, in the module `Examples` (`closure_of_two_has` to
  `static_closure_of_two_has_not`). The examples of before hold as
  they were.

## Agreed with other streams

With C1 (primitives; landed, `requests/C1-primitives.md`):

- The machine has `Value_block` and `Value_boxed_number`, and the
  observations `block_of`, `boxed_number_of` (only for the boxable
  kinds; the contents may be poison), `string_of`, `kind_of_value`.
  The meaning uses them.
- A closure and a boxed number are not blocks for `block_of`: the heads
  of kind value are disjoint, as `Meet` assumes
  (`observations_disjoint_statement`, the machine's).
- `relation_holds` is not defined by `eval_prim`. It is a relation of
  the types, wider than the primitives. `primitive_related` says that
  each relation contains the graph of its primitive.
- C1 flags, for the human (D6): a block made by `Make_block` and a
  symbol bound to an equal static block are different values. An alias
  type tells them apart; what is stated of lifting (T6) has to allow
  for that.

With C2 (kinds):

- The kinds are those of `FlambdaModel.v`; `kind : flambda_type ->
  flambda_kind`.
- `type_satisfied statics vars v t -> kind_of_value v = kind t`, with
  no premise (`type_satisfied_kind_statement`).
- A variable's kind comes from its binder, and C2's invariant of a
  state gives `kind_of_value` of its value: for T2's `env_satisfies`.
- Block shapes are defined here (`block_shape`); if terms come to need
  them they move to `FlambdaModel.v`. Value slots get kinds from a
  table given to C2's checker.

With C3 (sets of closures; landed, see "After the second epoch"):

- `function_slot : Type := positive` is in `FlambdaModel.v` under
  that name; mine went.
- C3 defines `closure_in_set statics v : option (function_slot * list
  (function_slot * code_id) * list (value_slot * value))` and
  `project_function_slot statics move_from move_to v : option value`.
  Until its epoch they were defined in `FlambdaTypeMeaningSpec.v`
  with the same names and types.
- Today's closures, whose function slot has no name, are read as the
  closure at `unnamed_function_slot` (C3 takes the constant into the
  semantics: my option (a)). The unnamed forms are meant to go later;
  nothing may depend on the constant being 1.
- `closure_in_set_slot_statement` (a closure is at a slot that its set
  has): C3 states and proves it in `FlambdaSemanticsSpec.v` under that
  name, with my text and no premise (its `closure_in_set` looks the
  slot up first, so a symbol bound to a static closure at a slot that
  its set has not is no closure). My copy went at C3's epoch.
- From C3: making a closure at a slot its set has not is undefined;
  `well_formed_unit` now implies that every static set is bound in
  every state of a run. If a statement about the `closure_types` of a
  symbol needs the sibling symbol to be the sibling closure, the
  premise to carry is `static_sets_bound statics`.
- C3's request is `middle_end/flambda2/rocq/requests/C3-closures.md` on
  its branch. From a symbol, `project_function_slot` gives the symbol
  that the set names for the slot, bound or not: the meaning of
  `closure_types`, which asks the type of the result, is right as it
  stands; only a claim that the result is a closure of the same set
  needs C3's premise `static_set_bound`.

With T2, T3 and T7 (types): they are cut from this branch and are

## Open questions

- Decided, D26 (the orchestrator, for the human, who may overrule):
  typing facts are about values that are not poison; see "After the
  first epoch". For T4: adding an equation whose meet is bottom on a
  name that holds poison needs the premise that the name is not
  poison.
- Ruled by the human, D8: any operation on a symbol that is not bound
  is undefined, and well-formedness rules such terms out. The
  `Is_null` relation relates an unbound symbol to 0, which is wider
  than the primitive and harmless. Still open: `Is_null` of poison
  (the relation says 0, the primitive has no value).
- For T4 and the human: the `Get_tag` case of
  `reduce_inverse_relations` deduces that a name is a block from a
  relation that unboxing records of names that may be immediates
  (T1-1, R3-2; in both implementations of meet). T4 has to choose: a
  model meet that deduces only what `relation_get_tag` gives, or a
  statement of meet with a premise for that case.
- D27, the two implementations of meet and join: the model is to end
  at the n-way one. T1-4 (the n-way join is not an upper bound on a
  function type that one input lacks) matters more for that; the run
  of the types library that shows it has been given to X2.
- For C0: `value_eqb` (an equality test of values, with its proof) is
  in `FlambdaTypeMeaningBoolSpec.v` for want of one in the machine. It
  goes by the constructors of `value` and has to gain a case with each
  new form; it should sit beside `value`.
- A constant `Tagged_immediate i` is an immediate for the types
  whatever `i` is; the machine's primitives have a value only on one in
  range (R3-15). Ruled (D32, D13): constants in range are to be a
  condition of well-formed terms, which closes it.
